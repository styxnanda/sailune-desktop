# Site watermark assets

- `ao3.svg`: Archive of Our Own mark from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/archiveofourown.svg), retrieved 2026-09-17. Simple Icons distributes its icon paths under CC0-1.0.
- `ffn.svg`: simplified vector adaptation of the white F monogram in FanFiction.net's favicon, already bundled in Sailune Desktop (`frontend/public/sites/ffn.ico`, originally https://www.fanfiction.net/favicon.ico). The colored document background is omitted for the watermark treatment.

The trademarks identify their respective sites and remain the property of their owners. They are bundled locally; rendering makes no network requests. Flutter applies low-opacity black in light mode and a pearl gradient in dark mode to both marks.
